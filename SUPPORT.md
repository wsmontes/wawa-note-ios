# Wawa Note Support

Wawa Note is a local-first iPhone app for recording, transcribing, scanning, importing, and organizing work into projects.

## Get help

- Email: [wawasoftbc@gmail.com](mailto:wawasoftbc@gmail.com)
- Report a reproducible problem: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/wsmontes/wawa-note-ios/issues)
- Privacy questions: see the [Wawa Note Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)

When reporting a problem, include the Wawa Note version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the smallest sequence that reproduces it. Do not attach recordings, transcripts, API keys, or other sensitive content unless you have reviewed and intentionally chosen to share them.

## Common actions

- Configure or revoke AI access: **Settings > AI Services**
- Review privacy status: **Settings > Privacy & Data Controls**
- Export your library: **Settings > Export Complete JSON**
- Restore an item: **Inbox > Trash**
- Permanently delete trashed items: **Inbox > Trash > Empty Trash**

Wawa Note has no account or cloud backend. Content stored only in the app cannot be recovered by Wawasoft after it is permanently deleted. Provider-retained copies must be managed with the provider you selected.
